Our Origins

The Hungry Mouse Story

How a passion for wholesome street flavors transformed into Lucknow's beloved Cloud Kitchen On The Move.

Born for Cravings, Built for Speed

The idea behind Hungry Mouse started with a simple observation: food lovers crave authentic, piping-hot food with consistent taste, without paying inflated restaurant dining markups.

We engineered a nimble, high-efficiency cloud kitchen model with mobile kitchen trucks that bring chef-crafted Indian, Chinese, Tandoor, and Continental treats right to where the city's appetite is highest.

Our Core Promise:

Every meal is prepared with zero compromise on freshness, packed securely in eco-friendly containers, and delivered swiftly to keep the flavors intact.
